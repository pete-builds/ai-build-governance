# Templates

The paperwork. **A completed template is the evidence** that a requirement was
satisfied, which makes these load-bearing even though they are not normative
text.

Often the most immediately useful part of the framework. If you are standing
this up, the inspection checklists are what you automate first.

## Two inspection templates for tool servers, and they are not interchangeable

| If you | Use | Why |
|:--|:--|:--|
| **Operate the server**, whether you wrote it or self-host someone else's | [MCP server inspection](inspection-mcp-server.md) | You can answer every question in it |
| **Do not operate the server**, because a vendor or third party runs it | [Third-party server boundary inspection](inspection-third-party-mcp.md) | The other template asks questions only an operator can answer, and answering them from vendor documentation produces false assurance |

[Chapter 13](../model/13-third-party.md) sets out the requirement, and
[MCP approvals](../guide/06-mcp-approvals.md) walks the decision.
